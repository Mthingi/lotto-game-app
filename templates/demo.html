MPG Technology Demonstration

Explore a technology demonstration of the digital customer journey being developed by My Phanda Game (MPG).

This demonstration illustrates the digital journey only. It does not process real lottery transactions or payments.

The intended service journey

1. Customer The customer accesses the service through a mobile device using USSD.
2. MPG Platform MPG provides the digital interface and customer journey between the mobile user and the relevant service.
3. Licensed Operator The intended model connects the digital distribution channel with a licensed lottery operator.
Prototype demonstration
This prototype demonstrates the proposed digital customer journey. Live payment, settlement and licensed-operator integrations are not enabled.
