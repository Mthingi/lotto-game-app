Expanding digital access through USSD

My Phanda Game (MPG) is a South African technology initiative developing a USSD-based digital lottery distribution channel designed to provide a simple mobile access point for lottery services.

Explore the MPG Journey Partnership Opportunities

Built for accessible mobile distribution

MPG is being developed around USSD technology, enabling a mobile-first customer experience without requiring a smartphone application or high-data connection.

How MPG works

MPG is being developed as a technology distribution channel connecting the customer journey with the appropriate telecommunications and licensed lottery operator infrastructure.

01

Customer

A customer accesses the service through a mobile USSD channel.

02

MPG Platform

MPG manages the digital customer journey and ticketing workflow through its technology platform.

03

Licensed Operator

The platform is being developed towards integration with the appropriate licensed lottery operator and telecommunications partners.

Why MPG

MPG focuses on creating a simple digital distribution layer around technology that is already widely accessible through mobile networks.

USSD-first

Designed around USSD to support mobile access without depending on a smartphone application.

Digital distribution

A technology channel designed to support the digital distribution of lottery services.

Partnership driven

MPG is being developed with the appropriate telecommunications, technology and licensed lottery partnerships in mind.

Partnership Opportunities

MPG is currently exploring opportunities with telecommunications operators, technology companies, VAS providers and licensed lottery operators.

The objective is to establish the appropriate commercial, technical and operational partnerships required to progress the platform towards production integration.
